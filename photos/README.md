# Real print photos

Photos are coming after physical testing. Current catalog images in
`renders/` show CAD meshes with schematic photo placeholders.

For each photo added here, record the filename, photographer/credit,
design and release version, print settings, and an explicit license or
permission statement. Photo rights are separate from the CC0 model files;
uploading a photo to an issue alone does not dedicate it to CC0.

Suggested permission for a freely reusable photo: “I took this photo and
dedicate it under CC0 1.0.” Alternatively, name a photo-specific license and
credit. Please have permission from identifiable people shown, including
people visible inside the framed instant photograph.
