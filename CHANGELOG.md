# Changelog

## v0.3.0 — Restored flower overlap — 2026-11-20

### Fixed

- Restored the source-art flower and ornament contours that cross the inner moulding onto the mat in **Canted Octagon, Cushion Cartouche, Floral Corners, Botanical Garland, Portrait Oval and Landscape Oval**. The earlier common footprint cut these features off at the mat boundary; saved relief fields also flattened that zone.
- Recovered the corresponding sculpted relief and added solid support down to the 0.68 mm mat. The photo aperture remains clear. No supports or brim are required by the supplied slices.
- Cleaned a microscopic contour coincidence in Portrait Oval so the restored extrusion remains a closed, watertight solid.

### Compatibility

- **Only the six affected fronts need reprinting to recover the detail. Reuse their existing matching backs.** The mats, matching backs, photo openings, sockets, clips, magnet pockets and photo insertion geometry retain their preceding geometry.
- Segmental Arch and Acanthus Gallery are unchanged. Keep every front with its own design's matching back.
- Previous prints remain usable. Cushion Cartouche and Landscape Oval lost only small edge details; the other four have more visible missing petals.

### Updated

- Canonical native 3MFs, aligned STL downloads, catalog hashes and slicer estimates.
- Actual-mesh front/angled previews, collection views, illustrated guide provenance and website download links.
- All-eight 3MF, STL, Complete and Two-Color P2S packs, with checksums and this upgrade information.
- Release checks bind exact models to successful two-plate slicing, restored-overlap extrusion paths, back toolpaths and compatibility comparisons. CAD inputs are cached for offline rebuilds.

### Validation status

The revised fronts passed digital geometry and Bambu P2S 0.4 mm PLA slicing checks. This release does **not** claim successful physical printing, surface quality or assembly testing of the revised fronts. Earlier Botanical Garland photographs show the pre-v0.3.0 geometry and are labeled accordingly. Revised app print profiles remain subject to matching real-print evidence; the editable raw projects are available now.

See the [upgrade guide and before/after views](docs/UPGRADING-v0.3.0.md). The [v0.2.0 release](https://github.com/obartra/baroque-instax-frames/releases/tag/v0.2.0) remains available for reproducibility.
