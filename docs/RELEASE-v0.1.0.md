# v0.1.0 — six-frame prototype collection

The first public release includes six Baroque-inspired Instax Mini fridge
frames: Canted Octagon, Segmental Arch, Cushion Cartouche, Floral Corners,
Botanical Garland and Acanthus Gallery.

Download **Baroque-Instax-Frames-v0.1.0.zip** below for the complete collection.
Each 3MF includes the multicolor front and its matching white back on two
plates. Start with **PLA-Clip-Fit-Test.3mf** before committing to a full print.

- Full, uncut 86 × 54 mm Instax Mini support.
- Even 6.5 mm inner white mats, just 0.36 mm high.
- Three concealed 6 × 3 mm magnet pockets per frame.
- Revised PLA snap forks with longer arms and rounded slot ends.
- Actual CAD renders, concept drawings, source scripts, build instructions,
  provenance and archived validation reports.

**Prototype status:** the projects passed computational geometry and Bambu
Studio slicing checks. These new designs and revised clips have not yet
been physically printed or durability tested. Photos and print reports will
be added after testing. The catalog pictures are renders.

The distributed 3MFs preserve the previously validated file hashes. Source
paths were made portable and build outputs are isolated in `build/`; a fresh
Segmental Arch source build was also checked during repository preparation.

Models and design assets: **CC0 1.0**. Code: **MIT**. See LICENSE and
PROVENANCE.txt. Earlier MakerWorld-derived remix files are excluded.

`SHA256SUMS` checks the individual projects. The `.zip.sha256` attachment
checks the complete archive. The release ZIP includes source and all assets;
GitHub's automatically generated source archives also contain the tracked
models, drawings and renders.
