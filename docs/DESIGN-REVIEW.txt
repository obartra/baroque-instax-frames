DESIGN REVIEW

For the later model-by-model historical and visual critique, including
terminology corrections and carving priorities, see HISTORICAL-REVIEW.md.

The family is held together by a white internal mat, a narrow sight-edge
roll, a recessed channel, a broad rising cove, fleshy leaf relief and an
opaque outer surround. The outer carving varies in density and balance.
The rectangles progress from quiet corner flowers to a botanical garland
to fuller acanthus. There are no identical repeated corner rosettes or
repetitive laurel stamps in the revised floral designs.

These are contemporary Baroque-inspired compositions. Historical research
supports architectural mouldings, corner/center ornament, foliate bunches,
and both quiet and densely carved frame treatments; it does not establish
that these new compositions are exact period reproductions.
Reference: National Portrait Gallery, Other studies — Northern European:
https://www.npg.org.uk/collections/research/programmes/the-art-of-the-picture-frame/other-studies-northern-european

FILM CONCEALMENT AND PICTURE RETENTION
The octagon clips small corners; the shallow arch clips only upper corners;
the cushion preserves most of a rectangular image while rounding corners.
All three retain about94% of the nominal image. The three rectangles retain
95.5%. The whole film is intact, and no white film is exposed beyond the
surround in the computed front projection. Wider corner lands improve
concealment efficiency compared with a pure elliptical outer contour.

The mat is a true6.5 mm parallel offset of the aperture, with deliberate
mitred corners for the rectangular/octagonal types. All four cushion corner
radii agree. Magnet locations are adjusted within the carving before
enlarging a whole frame. Final fronts are94–122 mm wide and about93–119 mm
high, depending on orientation; precise dimensions are in validation/.

MECHANICAL REVIEW
Each clip has a3.5 x2 mm stem with a1.4 mm round-ended slot; the two arms
are1.05 mm wide. The earlier arms were0.7 mm wide and1.4 mm deep. Tip height
increases from6.2 to8 mm. A3.88 mm head enters a3.65 mm socket neck: nominal
inward deflection is0.115 mm per arm, reduced from0.15 mm previously.
There is0.15 mm axial relaxation after seating. Flared bases and the
0.7 mm slot-end radius address the old sharp internal notch.
These are geometric changes intended to improve PLA durability, not a
measured fatigue or strength rating. Use the supplied1 g coupon first.

VISUAL QA
The first rectangular meshes exposed square clip caps in the architectural
corner moulding. The final rectangular snaps move outward behind the floral
masses and use rounded relief roofs, preserving a continuous inner moulding.
Actual mesh views are supplied alongside the design drawings so the
printable interpretation can be judged independently from ideal AI shading.
