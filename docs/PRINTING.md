# Printing and assembly details

Follow the [six-step illustrated guide](../README.md#print-and-assemble). The examples use actual silhouettes from the released Floral Corners and Segmental Arch projects. The same assembly sequence applies to the matching two-piece designs; keep each front with its own back.

## Materials and profile

| Item | Supplied project setting or requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Printer | Bambu P2S, 0.4 mm nozzle |
| Build surface | Textured PEI |
| Material | PLA in white and black |
| Layers | 0.08 mm; first layer 0.20 mm |
| Filament 1 | White |
| Filament 2 | Black |
| Plate 1 | One fused multicolor front: thin white mat, then black ornament |
| Plate 2 | Matching white back |
| Supports / prime tower | Disabled in the supplied profile |
| Magnets | Three round magnets, 6 mm diameter × 3 mm thick |
| Adhesive | Suitable for both your PLA and magnets; follow its preparation and curing instructions |

Open the `.3mf` **as a project** in Bambu Studio so its plates, parts and color assignments are retained. For another printer, choose the appropriate machine and review the resulting slice and clearances. Check purge requirements for your own multicolor setup.

The mat is **0.36 mm high**: a 0.20 mm first layer plus two 0.08 mm layers. Retain those white layers. The front then makes one white-to-black transition; the back stays white. The front is one fused piece, so there is no separate mat to glue or assemble.

## 1. Test the clips

Print **both plates** of the [PLA clip fit coupon](../models/PLA-Clip-Fit-Test.3mf), about 1 g in total. Align the socket squarely with the fork and press evenly. Test with the filament and profile you will use for the frame. If it needs excessive force or a fork cracks, resolve the fit before printing a complete front and back.

The revised forks have longer flex arms, broader sections, flared bases, round-ended slots and a smaller retention lip. These changes are intended to reduce brittleness. Physical fatigue life has not been established.

## 2. Print the matching pair

Print both plates from the same frame project. Plate 1 is the white-and-black front; plate 2 is its white back. Check the first layers and the sliced preview before printing. Earlier backs have different clips: do not mix generations or pair parts from different frame projects.

## 3. Fit the magnets

On the rear of the back piece, apply a small amount of suitable adhesive in each magnet pocket. The pockets are **6.3 mm diameter × 3.1 mm deep**, with a **0.5 mm closed floor**. Seat all three exposed magnet faces evenly at the rear plane, following your adhesive's instructions. Keep adhesive out of the photo channel, springs and snaps.

Let the adhesive cure fully **before assembly or inserting film**. Keep loose magnets away from children and pets. Do not use a part with an unsecured magnet.

## 4. Snap together while empty

Put the ornamental front face down on a soft cloth, with its socket side facing upward. Lower the matching back with its **four forks pointing down toward the sockets**. Align all four before pressing near each corner in turn. Support the part nearby rather than pushing on a projecting flower or leaf.

The photograph goes in **after** the front and back are joined. There are three guide sides and compliant edge fingers to locate the sheet inside the assembled channel.

## 5. Slide in the whole photo

Check insertion with a plain paper dummy first. The frame holds a complete **86 × 54 mm Instax Mini sheet**; its nominal image is 62 × 46 mm. Keep the instant film whole and do not force it.

| Orientation | Enter from | Leading edge | Wide caption border ends up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Landscape | Left | Narrow border | Left |
| Portrait | Bottom | Narrow border | Bottom |

Slide gently until the sheet reaches the firm stop. If it binds before reaching the stop, remove it and inspect the channel, adhesive and print quality. The paper border should sit behind the surround. Shaped openings deliberately mask parts of the image; their visible area is recorded in each design's geometry report.

These projects are for Instax Mini, not Polaroid Go, full-size Polaroid, Instax Square or Instax Wide film.

## 6. Place and change

Place the magnet side against the fridge or another suitable steel surface. To change the picture, gently slide it out through the same entry side. Adhesive hold, real film fit and long-term PLA clip behavior still need physical validation for these prototypes.
