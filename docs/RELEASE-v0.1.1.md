# v0.1.1 — clear free licensing and historical review

Download **Baroque-Instax-Frames-v0.1.1.zip** for the complete six-frame
collection, clip coupon, renders, drawings, source and instructions.

- Standard **CC0 1.0** text in the root LICENSE for GitHub recognition.
- Explicit **MIT** notices alongside the code and a separate LICENSING.md
  explaining the scope. Both are free-use terms, including commercial use.
- A research-informed historical and visual review of all six actual models,
  with museum sources, individual critiques and future carving priorities.
- More precise catalog wording: Baroque-inspired, with Rococo influences;
  contemporary designs rather than period reproductions.

**The seven 3MF files are unchanged from v0.1.0.** Their exact-file hashes and
archived validation results remain valid. The historical review records
recommendations; it does not claim that the sculptural revisions have been
implemented. Physical fit and durability of this collection remain untested.

The ZIP includes all assets and licenses. Its `.sha256` companion verifies
the archive; `SHA256SUMS` verifies individual models. Print the small clip
coupon first and keep each frame with its matching back.
