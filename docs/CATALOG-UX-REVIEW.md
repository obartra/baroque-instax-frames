- The assembly drawings explain forks down, front on a soft cloth, four joints, glue curing and photo loading after joining. Landscape entry from the left and portrait entry from below agree with the wide border trailing in each illustration.
- The example materials are consistent without prescribing color names. The fridge uses a small frame at plausible relative scale and a separately labeled enlarged detail, avoiding the previous oversized-frame impression.
- Prototype status, printer/profile assumptions and the difference between digital checks and physical validation remain available after the practical product flow.
