PROVENANCE — CURRENT EIGHT-FRAME CATALOG

The current catalog is catalog/current.json; source/designs-current.json
records the CAD and cached image/surface inputs. The current native projects
are under models/current/. The original six-model release is retained at its
original paths and has a separate provenance record in docs/PROVENANCE-v0.1.0.txt.

Current ornament comes from newly generated carving studies and custom CAD,
not from WLM files, biyou's MakerWorld mesh or another downloaded ornamental
mesh. Historical material input DID include The Metropolitan Museum of Art's
public-domain photograph of frame1971.206.34, design attributed to Meissonnier,
French ca1735. Its carving language informed the new drawings; the complete
outline and program are not reproduced literally. Getty's Louis Style: French
Frames1610–1792 informed research. See docs/CURRENT-CATALOG.md for direct links.

This differs from the first six text-led designs: the current masters have
historical image input. No personal photograph is used in this catalog.
The photo inside rendered frames is a synthetic CAD landscape placeholder.

The masters and generated surface-normal guidance were interpreted into
continuous supported relief using cached depth/normal-derived height fields,
local CAD refinements, exact photo apertures and independent moulding geometry.
This is not photogrammetry, a full multi-view reconstruction, a scan of an
antique frame or proof of historical workshop provenance. The miniature mat,
photo carrier, clips and magnet pockets are modern adaptations. Geometry is
uniformly scaled in XY; the ornament is not independently stretched by axis.

Botanical Garland's newest revision explicitly traces two unequal flowers into
supported CAD petal bowls, curved short overlap channels, rounded lips and low
irregular connected seed relief. The first revision's button-like centers and
doubled margins were rejected; v3 resolved those local requests. Independent
historian-role and product-design-role AI reviews accepted the actual v3 mesh
for a physical prototype. These are design reviews, not human authentication.

The backs and functional features are custom CAD primitives. Their dimensions
carry forward from this project's own photo-channel and clip work. The intended
PLA clips engage once and may be glued; repeated fatigue is not a release
claim. Print profiles are configuration data for local Bambu Studio, not a
redistribution of that application. Physical fit and durability need testing.
