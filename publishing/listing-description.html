Eight floral Rococo-inspired magnetic frames for full, uncut Instax Mini photos. Choose a rectangular, oval, arch, octagon or cushion silhouette; the film border stays hidden behind the frame and inner mat.

What you get

  • Eight designs: Canted Octagon, Segmental Arch, Cushion Cartouche, Floral Corners, Botanical Garland, Acanthus Gallery, Portrait Oval and Landscape Oval.
  • Two physical pieces per frame: a front with an integrated mat, plus its matching back.
  • Three concealed magnet pockets for magnetic surfaces.
  • Bambu Studio 3MF projects and separate STL parts for other slicers.
  • One color, or separate mat and frame colors. Reinforced 0.68 mm mats.

Photo fit

For Fujifilm Instax Mini: 86 × 54 mm full sheet, with a nominal 62 × 46 mm image. Insert the whole photo; do not cut instant film. The shaped opening crops some of the image, especially on the ovals. These files are not sized for Polaroid Go, full-size Polaroid, Instax Square or Instax Wide.

You need

Filament, three 6 mm diameter × 3 mm thick disc magnets per frame, and suitable glue. Magnet pockets are 6.3 mm diameter × 3.1 mm deep. Search magnets: https://www.amazon.com/s?k=6x3mm+neodymium+disc+magnets

Print and assemble

  1. Print the front and matching back on two plates. The supplied 3MF uses a Bambu P2S, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm first layer and 0.08 mm subsequent layers, with no supports.
  2. Glue three magnets into the back pockets; keep glue out of the photo channel.
  3. Align the four clips and snap the front and back together. The clips are intended for one assembly; they may also be glued.
  4. Slide in the uncut photo: landscape loads from the left when viewed from the front, portrait from the bottom. Insert the narrow-border edge first, with the wide caption border trailing.
  5. Place on a magnetic surface.

Stl users and two colors

Import mat and ornament together as parts of ONE object, preserving their original coordinates and heights. Do not center or drop those parts separately. Print the back as a separate object, flat side down and clips up. Units are millimeters.

Assign colors by part if your slicer supports multi-material printing. For a manual filament swap using the supplied layer schedule, change after layer 7 at Z=0.68 mm, before the first ornament layer at Z=0.76 mm. With another layer schedule, inspect the preview and switch at the first ornament layer. One-color printing needs no swap. Each STL package includes an import guide.

About the designs

These are new, historically informed compositions, developed with AI-assisted carving studies and custom CAD. They are not antique scans or reproductions. The current eight designs do not reuse the earlier WLM/MakerWorld ornamental meshes. Models and project artwork are released under CC0 to the extent rights can be waived; code is MIT. Detailed provenance and license scope are in the source repository.

Gallery images are CAD renders and assembly illustrations; the cover photo is simulated. Actual print photos will be added as they become available. The collection has digital geometry and slicing checks; this is not a claim that every design has been physically tested.
