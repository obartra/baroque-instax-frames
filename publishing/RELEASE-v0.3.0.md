# v0.3.0 — Restored flower overlap

Flowers and ornaments should flow across the inner moulding onto the mat. Earlier printable geometry cut them off at that boundary. This release restores the missing contours and sculpted relief, with solid support on the mat and a clear photo opening.

**Affected fronts:** Canted Octagon, Cushion Cartouche, Floral Corners, Botanical Garland, Portrait Oval and Landscape Oval. **Segmental Arch and Acanthus Gallery are unchanged.**

**Already printed one? Reprint only its affected front to recover the detail.** Existing matching back geometry is unchanged. Keep each front with its own design's back. Do not force apart glued joints or rely on damaged one-assembly PLA clips; print the supplied matching back again if needed. Previous fronts remain usable.

## Downloads

All four packs contain all eight designs:

- **3MF:** standard editable native projects, front and matching back on two plates, with example mat/ornament/back material slots.
- **STL:** aligned mat, front ornament and matching back parts, with import instructions. Import mat and ornament as parts of one object.
- **Complete:** native projects, STLs and current views.
- **Two-Color P2S:** full editable Bambu exports with two plate previews. Mat uses slot 1; ornament/back use slot 2. This is the easiest Bambu Studio starting point.

Supplied settings are Bambu P2S, 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA, 0.20 mm first layer then 0.08 mm, 0.68 mm mat, no supports or brim. Select your own printer/material and re-slice when different. `SHA256SUMS` covers all four exact archives. Changelog, upgrade notes, assembly illustrations and licensing accompany the downloads.

## Validation and limits

- Exact indexed-mesh comparisons retain the previous mat and matching back volumes, preserve existing ornament within numerical tolerance, and verify clear photo openings and mating/insertion geometry.
- The exact revised canonical files and all eight two-color exports pass two-plate slicing with one front material change, none on the back and no per-plate warnings.
- All 27 restored contour regions receive extrusion directly above the mat. Back toolpaths retain functional fork, magnet and spring regions at every checked layer.
- **Revised physical tests are pending.** No successful physical print, surface quality or revised assembly test is claimed by this release. Earlier real Botanical Garland photos show pre-v0.3.0 geometry. Revised app profiles require matching physical evidence; the editable raw projects are available now.
