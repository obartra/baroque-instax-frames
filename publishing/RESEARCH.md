# Listing research — 2026-11-17

The aim is clear discovery and easy printing, rather than copying another creator's wording or artwork. Engagement counts are snapshots, not proof that a presentation choice caused success.

## Applied to this collection

- Name Instax Mini explicitly; explain the 86 × 54 mm sheet and incompatible formats early.
- Lead with an actual-CAD frame render with a simulated photo; label it honestly.
- Follow with all eight designs, an angled depth view, rear film fit, and illustrated assembly.
- Explain three concealed magnets, two physical pieces, whole uncut film, reinforced mat, and one- or two-color options.
- Keep every design's matching parts together; retain native Bambu 3MF and portable STL downloads.
- Publish free under CC0; disclose AI-assisted ornament development. Preserve the separate MIT code license and third-party provenance scope.
- Replace or supplement the cover with real print photos when supplied. Do not label renders as print photos or imply that all eight designs have been physically tested.
